# Public product evidence review

Reviewed October 19, 2026. Primary sources were read independently; contributor history establishes personal responsibilities, while store records establish product history.

## Primary findings

- [Apple listing, version history and reviews](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/impella-app/id991345042): redesign note belongs to **3.3.1, March 6, 2023**; **3.3.2, March 10** lists fixes. Later entries include April 2023, September/November 2024 and December 2025. Visible feedback includes a positive layout comment (March 17, 2023), a video complaint (April 2021) and login eligibility concerns (March 9, 2023).
- [Google Play, package com.abiomed.impella](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.abiomed.impella): accessible under **Clinical Care**, retaining an Impella description. Shows **10K+** downloads and a December 15, 2025 update. Legacy reviews mention download/access problems in 2018; a 2022 review is positive. The download threshold covers the package’s lifetime, with no redesign cohort or active-user figure.
- [Abiomed Academy](https://content.abiomed.com/abiomed-academy-contact): continues directing visitors to the Impella app; useful distribution context, not a dated relaunch announcement.

## Interpretation and omissions

The iOS history documents a March 2023 redesign and subsequent releases. It does not establish an Android relaunch date, uninterrupted maintenance, implementation continuity or individual authorship. Store availability is public listing availability, not a completed installation or authenticated production inspection.

Targeted Abiomed, HeartRecovery and J&J MedTech searches did not locate a separate dated app-redesign announcement. No archived copy was needed to establish the accessible release history. Omit a claimed 2022 public relaunch, a March 10 redesign attribution, redesign-specific downloads, measured performance/cost/clinical outcomes, and claims that particular legacy failures were fixed. No aggregate satisfaction claim is warranted by the small mixed review sample.

Current descriptions support continuity of product capabilities; public evidence cannot verify that the private rendering engine is unchanged. The contributor’s recent observation of recognizable patterns is context, not an independently verified code comparison. No current production source, clinical formulas or user data were accessed for this research.
